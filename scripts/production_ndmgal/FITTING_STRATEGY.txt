NdMgAl11O19 — NLCE fitting strategy (triangle-based expansion)
================================================================

1) What the .dat files look like
--------------------------------
All curves are log-spaced in T with ~68–74 points / decade band.

0 T: Cm drops from ~2.9 J/mol/K at 0.06 K toward ~0.07 at 10 K. The
    ultra-low-T peak is dominated by hyperfine / crystal-field Schottky
    structure; NLCE truncation error is large below ~0.15–0.2 K (see prior
    campaign notes in configs/*.json).

1 T: A resolved Schottky bump: Cm rises to ~3.3 J/mol/K near 1 K then falls.
    This window (0.5–2 K in production configs) carries far more shape
    information per point than the 0 T tail.

3 T, 5 T, 7 T: peaks move to higher T and narrow; exchange sensitivity in
    the tabulated band is weaker than 1 T for the purpose of constraining
    frustrated-exchange models (also matches your phase-1 decision to drop
    them).

2) 0 T only vs finite field
----------------------------
* 0 T [0.2, 0.5] K alone: only ~7 points, mostly monotonic tail — good for
  checking the overall energy scale, weak for telling J1–J2 Heisenberg from
  bond-anisotropic models.

* 0 T + 1 T (recommended default in NdMgAl_production_0T_1T.json):
  - 0 T tail still anchors the low-T scale in an NLCE-convergent window.
  - 1 T Schottky adds amplitude + peak position + width → much stronger
    constraints on anisotropy and g_tensor usage (g_ab, g_c fixed from
    magnetization in the fitter CLI).

3) Model semantics in nlc_fit_triangular.py
---------------------------------------------
* --model xxz_j1j2
    Triangular-lattice Hamiltonian written in the J1 (nn) + J2 (n.n.n.)
    convention used by the NLCE runner, with optional --fit_Jz_ratio for
    XXZ anisotropy (Jxy/Jz).

* --model anisotropic
    YbMgGaO4-type bond-directional exchanges (Jzz, Jpm, Jpmpm, Jzpm).
    Setting Jpmpm = Jzpm = 0 recovers the “XXZ in anisotropic coordinates”
    slice used in your stage-1/2 XXZ scripts — NOT the same Hilbert-space
    Hamiltonian as xxz_j1j2 unless parameters map to a symmetry-related
    point.

4) Production workflow (recommended)
------------------------------------
A) Same NLCE order and resummation for both models (apples-to-apples χ²):
   ORDER=8, --resummation wynn_multi, --symm_threshold 9,
   --ed_method FULL (default in run_production_nlce_triangular_fit.sh) or
   AUTO once `qed` is rebuilt with CUDA on GPU nodes.

B) Run Bayesian fits with generous n_initial on the 4D anisotropic model;
   for xxz_j1j2 start with 2D (J1,J2) or 3D with --fit_Jz_ratio.

C) Compare best χ², residuals vs T per dataset, and physical plausibility
   (signs, magnitudes vs CW).

Use: bash run_production_nlce_triangular_fit.sh (see NdMgAl_NLCE/scripts).
